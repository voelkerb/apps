Privacy Policy

Brokon does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Network access

Brokon runs an MQTT broker on your device. Other devices on your network can connect to it, which is why the app asks for Local Network access. Only the clients you allow connect to your broker.

When your device is not on Wi-Fi, Brokon shows the address under which your broker can be reached. To find it, the app asks the public service api.ipify.org for your public IP address. The request contains no personal data beyond what any internet connection reveals. No other connections are made.

Data on your device

  • Broker settings are stored on your device.
  • Broker passwords are stored in the device Keychain and never leave the device.
  • Messages and logs are kept in memory. They are exported only when you choose to export them.

No tracking

Brokon contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
