Brokkr

A native MQTT broker for your Mac. Start, stop and manage local brokers.

About

Run one or more MQTT brokers directly on your Mac, with no setup. Handy for developing and debugging devices and apps on your own network. Pairs well with Brokio.

Features

  • Multiple brokersRun several brokers side by side, each with its own port.
  • MQTT 3.1.1 and 5Clients of both protocol versions can connect.
  • Local or networkListen only on this Mac, or open it to your network.
  • Access controlAnonymous or password login, plus per-topic allow and deny rules.
  • Live clientsSee connections, subscriptions and retained messages.
  • Event logFollow connects, subscriptions and publishes live.
