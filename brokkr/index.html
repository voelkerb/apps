Brokkr

A native MQTT broker for your Mac. Start, stop and manage local brokers.

About

Run one or more MQTT brokers directly on your Mac, with no setup. Handy for developing and debugging devices and apps on your own network. Pairs well with Brokio.

Features

  • Multiple brokersCreate and run several brokers side by side, each with its own name and port.
  • MQTT 3.1.1 and 5Clients of both protocol versions can connect, publish and subscribe.
  • Local or networkListen only on this Mac, or allow other devices on your network to connect.
  • User and passwordAllow anonymous access or require a login. Passwords are kept in the Keychain.
  • Topic access rulesAllow or deny read and write per user, per anonymous client or for everyone, with wildcards.
  • Live clientsSee connected clients, their subscriptions and message counts as they change.
  • Retained messagesBrowse retained messages and clear them with one click.
  • Last will and keep-aliveLast will messages are delivered and dead connections are dropped on timeout.
  • Event logFollow connects, subscriptions, publishes and errors in a live log.
