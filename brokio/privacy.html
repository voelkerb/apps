Privacy Policy

Brokio does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Network access

Brokio connects only to the MQTT brokers you configure, including brokers on your local network. This is why the app asks for Local Network access. No other connections are made.

Data on your device

  • Broker settings are stored on your device.
  • Broker passwords are stored in the device Keychain and never leave the device.
  • Messages and logs are kept in memory. They are exported only when you choose to export them.

No tracking

Brokio contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
